# 💰 OFFICIAL UKOMBOZINI DIVIDEND POLICY & FORMULA
**STATUS: FROZEN / ENFORCED**

This document outlines the **strictly enforced** mathematical logic for the Dividend Management Engine. These rules are hard-coded into the system and cannot be bypassed.

---

## 1️⃣ AVERAGE SHARES (AS)
The system calculates dividend entitlement based on a member's consistent contribution over time, not just their final balance.

### ✔ Formula
$$
\text{Average Shares (AS)} = \frac{S_{Jan} + S_{Mar} + S_{May} + S_{Jul} + S_{Sep} + S_{Nov}}{6}
$$

### 🔐 System Rules
- **Snapshots are Locked**: Balances are derived from immutable transaction logs.
- **Missing Periods**: If a member joins mid-year, their missing months count as **0**, reducing their average (Fairness Rule).
- **Integrity**: Snapshots are taken at the last second of the month (e.g., Jan 31st 23:59:59).

---

## 2️⃣ TOTAL REVENUE FUND (TRF)
Represents the **Gross Income** generated by the group.

### ✔ Formula
$$
\textbf{TRF} = I_{Banking} + I_{STL} + I_{LTL} + P_{Penalties} + O_{Other}
$$

### ❌ STRICTLY EXCLUDED
- Member Savings (Principal)
- Loan Repayments (Principal)
- Welfare Contributions
- External Project Funds

---

## 3️⃣ AVAILABLE PROFIT (AP)
Using Standard Accounting Practice for Net Surplus.

### ✔ Formula
$$
\textbf{AP} = \textbf{TRF} - \text{Expenses} - \text{Reinvested Loans / Reserves}
$$

### 🔐 Critical Rules
- **Negative Profit**: If $AP \leq 0$, the system **BLOCKS** dividend generation immediately.
- **No Distributions from Capital**: Dividends are paid ONLY from generated interest/surplus, never from member savings.

---

## 4️⃣ PROFIT TO SHARE OUT (PSO)
Determines how much of the profit is actually distributed vs. retained by the group.

### ✔ Logic
$$
\textbf{PSO} = 
\begin{cases} 
AP \times \mathbf{75\%} & \text{if Group Age} \geq 12 \text{ months} \\
AP \times \mathbf{50\%} & \text{if Group Age} < 12 \text{ months}
\end{cases}
$$

- **Group Age**: Determined automatically from the Group's registration date.

---

## 5️⃣ DIVIDEND RATE (DR)
The value of 1 Unit of "Average Share".

### ✔ Formula
$$
\textbf{Dividend Rate (DR)} = \frac{PSO}{\sum \text{Average Shares (All Members)}}
$$

### 🔐 System Rules
- **Division by Zero Protection**: If Total Average Shares = 0, Rate is 0.
- **Precision**: Calculated to 4 decimal places for accuracy.

---

## 6️⃣ MEMBER DIVIDEND (MD)
The final payout amount for a member.

### ✔ Formula
$$
\textbf{Member Dividend} = DR \times AS_{Member}
$$

## 🛑 SYSTEM SAFEGUARDS (AUTOMATED)

The Dividend Engine performs these checks automatically:
1.  **Snapshot Verification**: Verifies historical balances exist.
2.  **Profit Health Check**: Aborts if expenses exceed income.
3.  **Audit Trail**: Logs the user ID, timestamp, and precise calculation parameters used for the run.
4.  **Immutable Posting**: Once "Saved & Posted", dividends are credited to member accounts and cannot be regenerated for the same year.
