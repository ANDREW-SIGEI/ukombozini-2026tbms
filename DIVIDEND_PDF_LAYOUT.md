## 📊 DATA SECTIONS

### 1. Income Statement
Shows complete P&L breakdown:
- Total Income
- Total Expenses  
- **Gross Profit** (highlighted)
- Administrative Costs

### 2. TRF Deductions
Transparent breakdown of 15% policy:
- Mandatory Reserves (10%)
- Risk Buffer (5%)
- Reinvested Capital (optional)
- **Total TRF** (highlighted in orange)

### 3. Dividend Calculation
Step-by-step calculation display:
- Net Profit after TRF & Admin
- Share-Out Policy percentage
- Distributable Amount
- Total Average Shares
- **System-Calculated Dividend Rate** (highlighted in green)

### 4. Payout Summary
4-box visual summary widget:
- Eligible Members count
- Gross Payout amount
- Arrears Offset amount
- Net Payout amount (final distribution)

### 5. Formula Breakdown
Educational section explaining all formulas:
- TRF calculation
- Net profit calculation
- Dividend rate formula
- Member payout formula

### 6. Member Allocations
Complete table with 7 columns:
1. **#** - Sequential number
2. **Member Name** - Full name from database
3. **Avg Shares** - Calculated from snapshots
4. **Gross** - Before arrears (in green)
5. **Arrears** - Offset amount (in orange)
6. **Net Payout** - Final amount (in teal, bold)
7. **Posted** - Checkmark if posted to savings

### 7. Audit & Compliance
6 compliance checkpoints:
- System-generated calculations
- TRF policy enforcement
- Rate calculation transparency
- Automatic arrears deduction
- Immutability after posting
- Complete audit trail

### 8. Approval Workflow
3-step approval tracking:
- Calculated By (officer/system) + timestamp
- Approved By (director) + timestamp
- Posted By (director) + timestamp

### 9. Signatures
Physical signature areas for:
- **Director** - Name and date fields
- **Treasurer** - Name and date fields
- **Official Stamp** - Circle placeholder for org stamp

## 🎓 FORMULA REFERENCE

All formulas are clearly documented in the PDF:

```
1. TRF = Gross Profit × 15%
   ├─ Mandatory Reserves: 10%
   ├─ Risk Buffer: 5%
   └─ Reinvested Capital: Optional

2. Net Profit = Income - Expenses - Admin - TRF

3. Dividend Rate = (Net Profit × Share-Out %) ÷ Total Avg Shares

4. Member Dividend = (Avg Shares × Rate) - Arrears Offset
```
